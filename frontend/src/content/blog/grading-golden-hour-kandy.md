Late-afternoon light around Kandy Lake turns warm fast. Shot in S-Log3, the footage looks flat and grey until it is converted, and that conversion is where most grades go wrong.

## 1. Convert first

Apply the **S-Log3 → Rec.709** LUT at 100% to every clip from the same camera. Drag it onto the first clip, then select the rest and press Apply to selected.

## 2. Add the look at 70–80%

Drop **Kandy Golden Hour** on top and pull the strength down until skin stops going orange.

## 3. Finish with grain

A light **Film Grain 16mm** filter at 20% hides banding in the sky.
